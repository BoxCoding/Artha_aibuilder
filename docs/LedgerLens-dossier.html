LedgerLens

See what the statement isn't telling you.

A small business with no credit history has one thing to show a lender: a bank statement, and a statement can be edited in a browser in about a minute. LedgerLens reads the statement, checks the file itself for signs of editing, and gives a credit officer a recommendation in seconds, with a memo in which every sentence cites the row it came from. A person makes every lending decision.

Folio 00

Every line of the brief, and where it lives

The challenge asked for AI that detects fraud and risk, automates complex processes, improves customer interactions and personalises financial experiences, on Google Cloud. Each suggested direction and technology maps to working code. Google Cloud adapters are tested against mocked Google APIs; each has a local equivalent, so the same system runs on-premise with a local Gemma model.

Solution directions

✓
Fraud detection

Nine rules on the file itself: balance breaks, font changes, editing traces, prompt injection, date and reference anomalies.

✓
Transaction anomaly analysis

Outliers, circular transfers and window dressing inside a statement; the same statement, account or phone under different applicants, and application velocity, across the portfolio.

✓
Document intelligence

Readers for real Indian bank layouts that reconcile every balance, password-protected PDFs, Document AI for scans.

✓
Financial assistance

A customer coach in English, Hindi and Indonesian with a lending glossary; a credit co-pilot with read-only case tools.

✓
Personalised recommendations

An EMI date matched to the customer's cash-flow peak, structures the policy would approve, and concrete targets to reach a better band.

✓
Compliance automation

RBI-style Key Fact Statement with APR and an acceptance gate, AML watchlist and reportable-cash screening, a checklist that blocks non-compliant approvals, an auditor report.

✓
Risk assessment

Transparent scorecard and YAML policy, challenged by a learned model; an instant recommendation in about two seconds.

Folio 01

Deterministic code does correctness. Models do judgement. People decide.

Every boundary in the system follows from that sentence. A model may read, describe and explain. It may not compute an outcome, and it may not put a sentence in front of an underwriter that cannot be checked.

    Folio 05

    A reader that proves its own reading

    Indian statements come in dozens of layouts. The universal reader finds the table from its header words, assigns each word to a column by position, rejoins wrapped narrations, and reverses newest-first statements. Then it checks itself: a reading is trusted only if the running balance reconciles row by row.

    Header words become column roles

    dateTxn Datevalue date · ignoredValue DatenarrationDescription referenceRef No./Cheque No.debitDebitcreditCreditbalanceBalance

    Also handled: Withdrawal Amt. / Deposit Amt., a single Amount column with Dr/Cr, balances suffixed (Cr), dates as 01/04/26, 1 Apr 2026, 01-Apr-2026, and two-line headers such as "Withdrawal / (INR)".

    The self-check

    Each row must satisfy

    prev balance + credit − debit = balance

    One or two breaks are exactly what forensics exists to find, so the file is accepted and flagged. Many breaks mean the layout was misread, so the file goes to the model or a person instead of being scored wrongly.

    15-0718,240.002,05,480.00✓

    3,11,740 − 25,000 should be 2,86,740. The bank's own balance is 1,62,240: the deposit above was typed in.

    Folio 06

    Seventeen checks, three scopes

    They interrogate the file as an artefact rather than reading it as a document. Each emits evidence IDs that resolve to a box on a page. A serious finding sends the case to integrity review; nothing here ever declines anyone, and the customer never hears the word fraud.

    CRITICALFR-BAL-001

    Running balance does not reconcile, re-anchored per row so one edit is one break.

    CRITICALFR-FONT-001

    An amount cell set in a different font or size from the rest of its column.

    HIGHFR-META-001

    Producer names an editor, incremental re-saves, modified after issue. Per-bank producer profiles.

    HIGHFR-INJ-001

    Narration text addressed to an automated reviewer, redacted before any model sees it.

    HIGHFR-TXN-004

    Dates out of sequence or outside the printed statement period.

    MEDIUMFR-STAT-001

    Deposits far outside the account's own distribution, compared within category.

    MEDIUMFR-TXN-001

    Repeated bank reference numbers.

    MEDIUMFR-TXN-002

    Round-figure cash deposits in the final 15 days before applying.

    MEDIUMFR-TXN-003

    Money received from and returned to one counterparty within days.

    MEDIUMID-NAME-001

    Statement belongs to someone other than the applicant or their business. Triggers verification, not decline.

    Across the portfolio

    HIGHFR-XAPP-001

    The identical statement file was submitted by a different applicant. Compared as keyed hashes, never raw identifiers.

    MEDIUMFR-XAPP-002

    The same bank account appears in an application from a different PAN.

    MEDIUMFR-XAPP-003

    The same mobile number is given under a different PAN.

    MEDIUMFR-VEL-001

    Three or more other applications by the same PAN in 30 days: loan stacking or repeated attempts.

    Anti-money laundering

    HIGHAML-SAN-001

    The applicant or business name matches a sanctions or internal watchlist. Approval is blocked until compliance clears it.

    HIGHAML-SAN-002

    A counterparty named in the statement narrations matches the watchlist.

    MEDIUMAML-CTR-001

    Cash deposits of ₹10 lakh or more in a month: a reporting duty for the lender, not a finding against the applicant.

    Nothing is taken on trust

    Uploaded documents and model outputs are both treated as untrusted. The audit log is hash-chained and append-only inside the database itself, and the risk console recomputes the chain on every load.

    Accounts

    Argon2id passwords, lockout after repeated failures, sign-in limits per email and address, sessions revoked on sign-out, password change or disable.

    Customer data

    Aadhaar is never collected. PAN is masked in lists and absent from receipts. PII is masked before any model sees text. Statement passwords are used once and discarded.

    Documents

    Magic-byte, active-content and page checks, repeated after decryption. Pages render server-side, so the browser never parses an untrusted PDF.

    Models

    Figure whitelist, citation resolution, a deterministic critic, a groundedness gate and a quality gate. Removed claims are counted on screen, never hidden.

    Web

    Strict CSP with no inline script, Origin checks on writes, SameSite=strict cookies, HSTS in production, a request ID instead of a stack trace.

    Compliance

    A Key Fact Statement with APR on every approval and a customer acceptance gate before disbursal, a live checklist per case, and a one-click auditor report with the case's hash-chained trail.

    Operations

    Production refuses to start with a weak secret, demo mode, no database, an ephemeral receipt key or an in-process pipeline.

    Folio 11

    Before real money moves

    What is built is production-shaped. These are the gaps to close before customers use it.

    • Live Google Cloud. Every Google Cloud adapter is tested against mocked Google APIs. Run them once against a real project before relying on them.
    • Real statements. The universal reader is tested on layouts that imitate four banks. Run it over consented real statements from every bank you lend against.
    • Identity. PAN, GSTIN and Udyam are format- and checksum-validated only. Connect NSDL, GSTN and Udyam verification, e-KYC/CKYC and an Account Aggregator fetch, which removes the tampering question at source.
    • Model size. gemma3:1b drafts usually fail the memo quality gate, so the deterministic memo is used. Use gemma3:4b or larger, and a vision model for scans.
    • Credit model. Scorecard weights are hand-set and the challenger is trained on simulated outcomes. Refit on repayment data and test disparate impact by segment.
    • Access and regulation. Add MFA or SSO for staff, and review against RBI's digital lending directions, KFS requirements and the DPDP Act.
